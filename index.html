Sagip Pilipinas

A centralised disaster-response platform. Anyone can report a calamity in their area, and volunteer groups organise around that report to send relief where it is actually needed. I built the administrative side: secure user management and the dashboards that monitor activity across the system.

02 Live

Gourmet Gamble

A recipe platform built around a reverse search: enter the ingredients you already have and it suggests what you can cook, which cuts food waste. Around that sit user profiles, a community feed, ratings and a cooking chatbot. I ran this one as Team Lead and QA Specialist, holding the documentation and the testing standard while the team shipped.

  • React.js

03 Thesis

Project TIDE-Y

A solar-powered beach-cleaning robot, built as a proof of concept against marine plastic pollution. It navigates inside a set boundary, avoids obstacles, and uses a camera with machine learning to sort plastic and glass into separate compartments. Tidal data schedules its runs for low tide.

  • Machine learning
  • Robotics
  • Solar power

About

A developer who thinks like a tester.

Short version: I write the code, then I try my best to break it.

I'm Arbien M. Armenion, a Computer Engineering graduate from Cebu Institute of Technology – University, and I build web applications. Most of my work lives in JavaScript and TypeScript, with React and Next.js on the front end and Firebase or Supabase behind it.

Before that I spent a 240-hour internship in quality assurance at Arielus Software Inc. Six weeks is not long, but it rewired how I build. I spent it testing systems for user data traffic capacity, writing error logs precise enough that a developer could act on them without asking me a single question, and sitting with those developers while the fixes landed.

That is the habit I kept. I write test cases before I trust a feature, I assume every input field will eventually receive something ridiculous, and I check my own work the way I would check someone else's. On Gourmet Gamble I did that as Team Lead and QA Specialist, holding the documentation and the quality bar in place while the team shipped.

I am looking for a software developer role where writing the feature and hardening it are the same job.

FAQs / Contact

Work worth building. Code worth trusting.

Tell me what you are building and where it is breaking.

01 What do you actually build?

Web applications, front to back. React, Next.js and TypeScript on the front end, Firebase or Supabase behind it. I also take testing-only work on builds that are already someone else's.

02 How fast can you start?

I am available now. A small fix or a testing pass can usually begin within a couple of days. A full build needs a short scoping conversation first, so figure on starting inside a week once we agree what it is.

03 How much do you charge?

It depends entirely on scope, so I quote per project rather than publish a rate. Tell me what you need and roughly when you need it, and I will come back with a fixed price and a written list of what that includes. If the scope is still fuzzy, I will suggest a small paid discovery step first so neither of us is guessing.

04 How long do you usually reply to emails and messages?

Within 24 hours on weekdays, and usually the same day. If a question needs a longer answer than I can give straight away, I will still acknowledge it so you are not left waiting in silence.

05 Can you work with teams of developers?

Yes, and I have. On Gourmet Gamble I was Team Lead and QA Specialist, aligning tasks across the team and keeping the documentation and quality standards in place. During my internship at Arielus Software Inc. I worked next to developers daily, logging defects and following each one through to its fix. I work in Git and GitHub throughout and leave handover notes, so picking up after me is not archaeology.
